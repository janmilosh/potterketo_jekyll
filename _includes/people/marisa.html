Marisa Armeno, MD, PhDc

Marisa is a physician specializing in clinical nutrition, focusing on ketogenic therapies since 2010. She has provided nutritional treatment for adults and children with obesity and a variety of neurological disorders, including epilepsy, GLUT1-DS, PDCD, and psychiatric conditions. She is passionate about optimizing health with nutritional interventions and studying metabolism. Marisa has recently completed her PhD in ketogenic therapies with a focus on new clinical applications. She is currently training and educating professionals in therapeutic ketogenic therapies.

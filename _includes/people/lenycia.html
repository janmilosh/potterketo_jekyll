Lenycia Neri, PhD, MSc, RDN

Lenycia is a Brazilian Registered Dietitian with over 13 years of experience in Nutrition Outpatient Care at the Children’s Institute, São Paulo, Brazil. With extensive expertise in ketogenic metabolic therapies, clinical nutrition, public health, and pediatric dietetics, Lenycia has authored several books and created a Postgraduate Course in Maternal Infant Clinical Nutrition. Currently, she is a Research Fellow at the University of Pavia, Italy, where she studies the applications of ketogenic diet therapies in epilepsy, migraines, psychiatric conditions, and other areas. Lenycia is passionate about caring for people and their families, supporting them in rediscovering the joy of living well. View CV

Monica McChesney, MS, RDN, LD

Monica has been a pediatric clinical dietitian at Children's of Alabama for the past 8 years. She has worked in a variety of settings including inpatient rehabilitation and gastroenterology, but her primary focus is in neurology, specializing in the ketogenic diet. She has worked with a variety of neurological disorders, including epilepsy, glucose transporter type 1 deficiency syndrome, pyruvate dehydrogenase complex deficiency, along with many others. She has a true passion for the diet, as well as for the patients and families she serves. View CV

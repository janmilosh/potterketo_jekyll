Christine Wheeler, MS, RDN, CSP

Christine is a Registered Dietitian with board certification in pediatric nutrition. She has worked in the nutritional management of children and adults with a variety of healthcare concerns, specializing in ketogenic therapies since 2010. She is passionate about optimizing health with nutrition and other lifestyle factors. Christi was the Primary Investigator and author of Efficacy and tolerability of a whey-based, medium-chain triglyceride enhanced ketogenic formula in children with refractory epilepsy: A retrospective study.

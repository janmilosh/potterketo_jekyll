Erin K. Waranch, MS, RDN, LD

Erin has been a pediatric clinical dietitian for the past 7 years. She specializes in ketogenic diet therapy for conditions like pediatric epilepsy, infantile spasms, glut-1 deficiency and pyruvate dehydrogenase deficiency. Erin enjoys individualizing nutrition plans for her patients to help them be as successful as possible.
